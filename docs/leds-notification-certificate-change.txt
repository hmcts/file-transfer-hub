WHAT IS THE LEDS FTPS GATEWAY?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

The LEDS FTPS Gateway is an HMCTS-operated FTPS service that allows your systems to
securely transfer files to His Majesty's Courts and Tribunals Service. Your server
connects to the HMCTS LEDS FTPS Gateway endpoint at {{PUBLIC_ENDPOINT}} using
implicit FTPS (port 990) and authenticates with a username and password. HMCTS then
forwards the transferred files to their destination within the HMCTS estate.
